How audio works in the app

Each Malayalam character needs one audio recording. The app reuses the same files across all task types:

  • Discrimination: plays two character recordings in sequence — same char twice (SAME), or two different chars (DIFFERENT). No extra files needed.
  • Identification: plays the prompt character's recording.
  • Matching: plays the recording when a sound card is tapped.
  • Transliteration / Handwriting: plays the character's recording on demand.

→ Record one clear audio file per character. The app handles all the rest.

What to record for each character

  • Vowels (a, ā etc.): Pronounce the vowel sound clearly — short and isolated. For long vowels (ā, ī…), hold the sound visibly longer than the short version.
  • Consonants (ka, ma etc.): Malayalam consonants carry an inherent 'a'. Record the full syllable — e.g. ka, not just k.
  • Syllables (kā, mā etc.): Record the full syllable as spoken — kā with a long ā.
  • Discrimination pairs: The app plays your existing files back-to-back. Make sure the contrast between short/long vowels (a vs ā) is clearly audible in your recordings.
